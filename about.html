We don’t chase trends; we set the standard. Our development process is raw, calculated, and uncompromising. From the first sketch to the final heavy-stitch, we build garments that command attention and stand the test of time.

Heavyweight Fabrics: We source ultra-premium, high-GSM fabrics and raw textiles that actually have weight, texture, and a premium structure. Precision Engineering: Every fit is aggressively tested. We re-engineer silhouettes down to the millimeter to ensure an imposing, perfect drape on the body. Flawless Finish: Reinforced stitching, custom hardware, and industrial-grade quality control. If it isn't perfect, it doesn’t leave the floor.

Behind the label is a collective of absolute specialists. We don’t just hire personnel; we bring together master tailors, aggressive designers, and textile perfectionists who live and breathe this culture. Elite Designers: Driven by sharp, forward-thinking creatives who know exactly how to blend subculture roots with high-end luxury aesthetics. Master Craftsmen: Our tailors and pattern makers possess decades of technical expertise, turning complex structural concepts into wearable art. The New Guard: A team completely locked into global street culture, ensuring our drops are always ahead of the curve and culturally relevant.

We don't flood the market, and we don't do filler. Our strategy revolves around high-impact, limited-run releases. We believe in absolute exclusivity, premium value, and sustainable dominance. Curated Drops: We release limited, hyper-focused seasonal collections. When it's gone, it's gone. No overproduction, no dead stock. Direct & Unfiltered: By cutting out corporate middlemen, we invest every single rupee directly back into premium materials and superior craftsmanship. The Long Game: We build timeless, heavy-duty essentials designed to anchor your wardrobe for years, defying the fast-fashion disposable loop.
